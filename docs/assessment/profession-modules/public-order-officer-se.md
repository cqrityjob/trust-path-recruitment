# Public Order Officer – Sweden (Ordningsvakt) — module blueprint

**Slug:** `public-order-officer-se` · **Market:** SE · **Legally regulated:** yes
**Validation status:** `design` — no items authored, not assignable.

Ordningsvakt carries specific legal powers under Swedish law. Any item touching mandate, powers, obligations or legal terminology requires `legal_basis_required = true`, a named legal source, a reviewer, a review date and an expiry. Swedish requirements do not apply automatically in any other market.

| Teamwork and coordination | SCC-08 |

| Documentation and post-incident actions | SCC-06, SCC-09 |

Spec 5.2 gives this role the highest indicative relevance on SCC-04, SCC-05 and SCC-11 (all 12) — decision-making under pressure, emotional regulation and proportionality. Item coverage should reflect that emphasis once weights are validated.

## Targets

~48 draft items in the bank; ~24 in the first operational form; 12–15 minutes.

## Boundaries

The module measures judgement and behaviour, not legal expertise and not physical intervention capability. It is not security vetting, and it must never produce a claim about a candidate's legal suitability for the role — that is an authority's decision, not an assessment's.
