## Root cause and fix
The header count, step chip, pager and rows come from ONE response
(listRecruitmentCandidatesPage), which on this case evaluates every
application (section 3). The spec asserted at page load with the default 5 s,
racing that read; on the loaded CI runner the read finished just after the 5 s
(the screenshot shows the count), on #473 and 6264485 just before.
The spec now waits for that response before asserting (openCaseRead /
candidateRead) -- the event the assertion is about -- and asserts at once; no
assertion timeout is raised. The read's cost is a separate product finding
(per-row permission checks in rec_ri_candidate_view), not changed here.
