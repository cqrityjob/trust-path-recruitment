# CQrityjob certification research catalogue — 2026-10-03

## Deliverable
170 researched programmes from 40 issuers, with 117 distinct source URLs. This is a broad, curated first version, not every credential worldwide. It includes internationally offered certifications and internationally relevant qualifications tied to particular professional or national systems. Recognition, programme existence, accreditation and legal work eligibility are different questions.

Files: certifications.sqlite (relational research database), catalogue.json (complete interchange), credentials.csv (flat UTF-8 BOM interchange), validation.json (counts and integrity checks), DATA-DICTIONARY.md (field meanings). The companion Excel file provides filters and manual decision fields. The database contains no people, candidate claims, identity numbers or documents.

Research areas: cyber/privacy/IT audit 46; insurance/claims 30; physical security/investigation 25; resilience/continuity/fire/occupational safety 32; risk/compliance/fraud 37.

## Research limitations and follow-up
- 153 rows have direct official-page evidence, 5 use issuer-owned badges and 12 rely on official search excerpts. Search excerpts are provisional; 13 rows total are marked source_recheck_required, including transitional programme wording.
- Most sources establish programme existence. Accreditation, entry requirements, exam languages, fees, renewal details and territorial recognition were not exhaustively audited. Renewal notes are narrative, not executable rules.
- CILA Advanced Diploma transition, PRMIA ORM naming, OSCP/OSCP+ versioning and membership-dependent insurance designations need particular care. Follow row limitations.
- Course/issuer directories change. A future refresh must record changes and aliases without overwriting existing candidate history.
- The catalogue is intentionally not a list of local security licences, visas, memberships, organisational ISO certification, product certifications or course attendance badges. These need separate data models if requested.
- Further discovery can cover additional maritime/aviation security, executive protection, industrial safety, healthcare risk, actuarial qualifications and regional insurance institutes. Add only after official programme checks; do not fill a quota with unverified names.
